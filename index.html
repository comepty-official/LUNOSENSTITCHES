Good to know

Frequently
asked.

Do you make custom designs?+

Yes. Share your preferred design or discuss your idea with us during a consultation.

How long does an order take?+

Scrubs and kids' wear usually take 5 — 10 working days. Women's wear and occasion pieces take 2 — 3 weeks. Bulk orders are scheduled at confirmation.

Can I choose my fabric and colour?+

Absolutely. We'll guide you through suitable fabric, colour, and finishing options for your piece.

Do you make bulk scrubs?+

Yes. We welcome team and department orders from 10 sets upwards, with a reduced price per set. Use the booking form and select Bulk Order so we can prepare a quote.

Do you offer delivery?+

Delivery and collection options are available. We'll discuss the best option for your location at confirmation.
